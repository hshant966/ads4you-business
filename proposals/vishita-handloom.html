How GMB Works

The 6-Phase Google Business
Optimization Process

Exactly how we optimize your Google Business Profile — step by step.

1
Setup & Verification
Day 1–3
  • Claim or create your GMB profile
  • Video verification — show storefront live to Google
  • Add exact legal business name
  • Enter full address with Latur PIN code
  • Set accurate business hours & weekly off day
2
Category & Description
Day 3–5
  • Primary category: Clothing Store / Textile Store
  • Secondary: Saree Store, Handicraft Store, Cotton Fabric Store
  • 750-character keyword-rich business description
  • Include: handloom, Latur, natural dye, handmade, cotton
  • Add all attributes: women-friendly, in-store shopping etc.
3
Photos & Products
Day 5–7
  • 15+ photos: storefront, interior, products, textures
  • Cover photo: best-selling saree or collection
  • Add 10–15 products to Google with photos
  • Products sync to Google Shopping automatically
  • Services section: list all offerings in detail
4
Q&A Seeding
Day 7
  • "Do you sell handmade sarees in Latur?" → Yes...
  • "Are your fabrics natural and chemical-free?" → Yes...
  • "Can I inquire on WhatsApp?" → Yes, [number]
  • "What is the price range of sarees?" → [Your range]
  • Include "Latur" naturally in all answers for local SEO
5
Google Posts (Weekly)
Ongoing
  • Product post: new arrival saree with WhatsApp link
  • Educational post: "Why handloom costs more"
  • Offer post: festive / seasonal promotions
  • Behind-the-scenes: weaving process, natural dyes
  • 4 posts/month keeps profile active & ranked
6
Reviews Strategy
Ongoing
  • Create a short Google review link for easy sharing
  • Ask 5–10 existing happy customers to review
  • Respond to every review within 24 hours
  • Target: 4.0+ star rating (required for "best" searches)
  • Businesses responding to reviews are 1.7× more trusted
